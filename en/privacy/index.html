Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This page explains the contact options on this website and how the information you choose to share may be used.

Information you choose to share

If you contact us, you may share your name, contact details, location and project information. We use that information to discuss your request and communicate with you.

How this website works

The estimate form prepares a WhatsApp message or email on your device. It does not send information to a website database. Your selected language is remembered in your browser's local storage. No analytics or advertising scripts are added by this website.

External services

Phone, email, WhatsApp and Viber links open external apps or services. Those providers and the hosting provider may process technical information under their own privacy policies. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your enquiry.

Questions and updates

For questions about information you have shared, contact ups.rebuild@gmail.com. This is a starting policy for the current website, not legal advice. The company should review and update it before adding analytics, form services or other data processing.
